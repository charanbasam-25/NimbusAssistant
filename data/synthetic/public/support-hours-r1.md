Nimbus Cloud support handles account and deployment questions through the authenticated workspace dashboard. The support schedule below applies to standard requests for hosted FastAPI services; a published incident notice may provide separate updates during a platform event. When opening a case, include the workspace name, the affected service, the time the issue began, and the result the account owner expected.

For workspaces in region R1, standard support coverage runs from 08:00 to 20:00 Eastern time on business days. Cases submitted outside those hours remain in the queue for the next staffed period unless an active service notice describes a different response process. The dashboard records the time a case was submitted and shows its current status.

Do not post credentials, customer payloads, or payment details in a public discussion. If the request concerns an outage, state the user-visible impact and any temporary workaround already tried. Account owners can add updates to the existing case instead of opening duplicates, which helps support keep the investigation history together. These fictional service terms do not promise emergency coverage or replace a real contract.
