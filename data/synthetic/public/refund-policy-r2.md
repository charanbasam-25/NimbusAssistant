Nimbus Cloud account owners can request a refund for an eligible new subscription through the Billing page in the workspace dashboard. The request should identify the subscription and include the invoice reference, purchase date, and reason for the request. Support uses those details to locate the transaction; payment-card or bank-account numbers should never be copied into a support ticket.

Refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of purchase for a subscription in region R2. The submission date is the date the account owner opens the request in the dashboard, not the date support finishes reviewing it. The billing team confirms eligibility and sends its decision to the account contact. An invoice marked paid does not by itself mean a refund has been approved.

If the account owner cannot access the Billing page, they should open a support case from the authenticated workspace and provide the workspace name and invoice reference. Requests concerning a renewal, add-on, or disputed charge should include the relevant billing period so the team can review the correct transaction.
