This runbook guides the response lead when a confirmed incident affects a Nimbus Cloud hosted FastAPI workspace. Verify the scope and customer impact, open the internal incident record, and use the approved status channel. The first message should state what is affected, what mitigation is underway, and when the next update is expected. Mark estimates clearly and avoid describing an unverified cause as fact.

For an R1 incident, the response team must send its initial notification within 15 minutes of confirming impact. Record the notification time and link to the incident record so another responder can continue the communication. If the situation changes, publish a correction that preserves the earlier update for audit review.

Do not include secrets, private payloads, or customer data in a status message. Use a service-level summary and direct account-specific questions to the authenticated support case. The incident lead should assign an owner for the next update and keep the internal timeline current. This fictional runbook is training material and does not establish a real service commitment.
